1. About PASS

PASS is a one-phone, in-person party game app. It offers local questions, challenges and multiplayer turns in English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. No PASS registration or account is required. Game prompts and music are bundled with the app; advertising, consent and store purchases can communicate online. A Google Play account may be needed for purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

PASS saves player names or nicknames, language selection, sound and haptics choices, onboarding/help completion flags, and a local Remove Ads entitlement flag using device preferences. Names identify players in games; settings restore your choices after restart. You may use nicknames rather than legal names.

PASS does not send this player list or your answers to a developer server or supply the names to advertising requests. It does not record spoken answers, private chats, photos or contacts. Current rounds, random-player pools, bomb numbers, prompt history and ad-frequency counts are held in memory rather than saved as a game-history profile. There is no separate Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or Sentry integration in PASS; this does not exclude Google's advertising diagnostics.

Device backup or transfer features may copy app data, depending on operating-system and account settings. PASS currently has no explicit Android backup exclusions. Local storage therefore does not mean an absolute guarantee that data can never leave the device. See Android backup information.

3. Advertising and consent

PASS integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. The current version uses Google's official test ad IDs; production advertising has not been configured. Test ads do not establish that the SDK makes no network requests.

Google's SDK disclosure identifies IP-derived general location, device/account identifiers, interactions and diagnostics as advertising-related data. Actual handling depends on SDK version, choices and configuration; it is not necessarily anonymous. See Google's SDK disclosure.

PASS uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information and display a form when required. Advertising requests depend on the permission status returned by UMP. Settings shows Privacy Options when UMP requires that entry point. Availability and the choices shown depend on region and the configured Google message. Consent errors do not prevent local gameplay. Consent refusal does not necessarily prevent every Google request or every form of limited advertising. See UMP documentation.

Android advertising-ID controls are also available in device settings. Remove Ads prevents PASS interstitial ad loading/display once the entitlement is active; it does not promise that Google consent, billing or SDK initialization stops entirely.

If the consent update, required form or necessary advertising configuration fails, PASS does not load interstitial ads for that failed flow; local gameplay continues. A refusal that makes UMP disallow ad requests also prevents loading. Non-personalized advertising still involves Google data processing and may require consent.

4. Purchases and third-party services

Remove Ads is implemented as a non-consumable store purchase. Its production availability, price and store configuration still require confirmation. Android payments and restoration use Google Play Billing; PASS does not collect payment-card details. Purchase status and verification data are received through the store SDK to process the entitlement and complete purchases. The app's custom persistent storage saves an entitlement boolean, not a payment-card record or raw receipt.

Google processes its own advertising, consent and billing information under its policies, which may involve international processing. Google retains records under its own rules; PASS cannot promise to erase Google's purchase or advertising records by clearing app storage. Read Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

5. Retention and your controls

Player names and saved settings remain until changed or cleared. Edit Players changes the list; New Group clears the saved player list but does not reset every preference, consent state or purchase history. Android's Clear storage or uninstall removes local app data, subject to backup/restoration behavior. Temporary gameplay state lasts only for the current running session.

Use Settings for language, sound, haptics and available privacy choices; use Google/device controls for Google's information. Clearing a local entitlement does not cancel a purchase. Restore Purchases can restore an eligible store entitlement. For privacy questions or applicable access, correction, deletion or objection requests, contact innnx.dev@gmail.com. Requests concerning Google's independent records may also need to be addressed to Google. No fixed Google retention period is promised here.

6. Younger users and intended audience

PASS is intended for people aged 16 and over (16+), including 16–17-year-olds. This is the developer's intended audience, not an official content rating approved by Google Play or IARC. The final store rating remains to be determined through the store's rating process. PASS is not intended for users under 16; this statement does not mean the app verifies or enforces every participant's age.

A 16+ audience still includes minors. Reaching a digital-consent age does not automatically make a user eligible for personalized advertising. PASS does not verify each participant's age. As a conservative shared-device measure, its current code requests non-personalized ads, configures teen age treatment and a G advertising-content ceiling before explicit advertising SDK initialization, and checks that configuration before loading ads. These settings do not establish legal compliance or an official game content rating. Production account settings, regional applicability and real-device behavior still require review. Participants may skip prompts and should respect each other's boundaries.

7. Security, updates and contact

PASS relies on operating-system app storage protections; it does not add custom encryption to saved preferences. Google's advertising documentation describes TLS transport, but not every network transmission has been independently verified. No system provides an absolute security guarantee.

We will update this policy to reflect material changes and show the effective date above.
